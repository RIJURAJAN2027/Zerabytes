About us

We started Coffee Sagaa because we wanted the filter coffee we grew up with, made fresh every time and poured with a bit of a show. The menu grew from there: buns, momos, Maggie at midnight. It's a small place, the staff know the regulars, and nobody rushes you out.

  • Decoction made through the daySo your coffee never tastes like it's been sitting there.
  • Momos folded in our kitchenSteamed, fried, peri peri or cheesy.
  • Sugar, jaggery, honey or sugar-freeYour choice, no extra charge.
