// Our saga

Every cup here is a little saga. Beans roasted dark and ground fine. Decoction dripped slow through a brass filter. Milk boiled till it froths, poured from a height between tumbler and davara. That's how Chennai has always done coffee — we just kept the ritual, and added a few buns, momos and late nights to it.
